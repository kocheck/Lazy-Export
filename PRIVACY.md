# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 17, 2026

## Overview

Lazy Export ("The Plugin") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The Plugin operates entirely locally within your Figma environment and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, design data, or usage analytics to external servers.

## Data Collection & Usage

- **No Data Collection:** We do not track how you use the plugin.
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not access or store your name, email, or Figma user ID.
- **No Design Data Exfiltration:** Your design tokens, layer names, and exports are processed locally on your machine and are never sent to a cloud server.

## Permissions

The Plugin runs within Figma's secure sandbox environment. It does **not** request or utilize the following sensitive permissions:

- **Camera & Microphone:** Access is explicitly blocked via our permissions policy.
- **Geolocation:** Location data is never accessed.
- **Network Access:** The Plugin does not make external network requests, except for standard Figma asset loading (e.g., user profile images if applicable, though currently unused).

### Why do I see "Permissions Policy Violation" warnings?

You may notice warnings in the developer console (e.g., regarding camera or microphone). These are generic warnings generated by the browser environment when an iframe is created without explicit permission grants. **These warnings verify that the permissions are BLOCKED, not that they are being used.** We have added explicit headers to confirm our intent to never use these features.

## Third-Party Services

The Plugin has no external dependencies that track you. We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or any other tracking software.

## Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/kocheck/Lazy-Export).
